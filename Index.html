About The Project - Fasal Guru / Fasal Vaani

Built for SIH 2026
Topic: Early Detection and Management of Crop Diseases and Pest Infestations

Problem Statement

Farmers in India lose 20-30% of their yield every year due to late detection of crop diseases and pest attacks. They lack access to instant, expert advice in their own language, leading to wrong pesticide use, increased cost, and poor soil health. There is a need for a single, simple, AI-powered tool that can diagnose problems early and guide them with actionable solutions.

How Fasal Vaani Helps

Fasal Vaani is an AI-powered farming assistant designed to be a 24/7 guru for every farmer. It has 3 core features:

1. Leaf Analyzer 🌿

Just upload a photo of the affected crop leaf. Our AI detects the disease/pest instantly, identifies its severity, and provides step-by-step treatment and prevention tips.

2. Soil Analyzer 🌱

Analyzes soil health parameters and suggests what your soil needs. Get personalized recommendations to improve fertility and cure deficiencies before it affects your crop.

3. AI Chat Bot - Fasal Guru 🤖

A real-time farming expert in your pocket. Chat with our AI about any farming doubt - from weather to mandi prices to organic farming.

Language Independent

Works seamlessly in 3 languages - English, Hindi, and Bengali - so every farmer can get help in the language they are most comfortable with.
